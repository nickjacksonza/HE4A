Ace Robinson, M.P.H., M.H.L., has spent two decades building the teams behind HIV, mpox and public health programs. He serves as Acting Director of Duke University’s Sexual & Gender Minority Wellness program, co-chairs the Federal AIDS Policy Partnership, sits on the UCLA CHIPTS steering committee, and chairs the Brown University Advisory Council to Eliminate Anti-Black Racism.
